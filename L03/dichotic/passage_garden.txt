My grandmother has a small garden behind her house. She grows tomatoes, beans,
and many kinds of flowers. Every morning she waters the plants before
breakfast. She says the soil must never get too dry. In spring, the garden is
full of colour. In summer, she picks the tomatoes and makes soup. She gives
some to her neighbours. They often give her fruit in return. In autumn, the
leaves fall and she sweeps them into a big pile. In winter, she reads books and
plans next year's garden. She says the garden teaches her to be patient.
