I walk to work every morning. The walk takes about twenty minutes. I pass a
small park and a bakery. The bakery smells wonderful. Sometimes I buy fresh
bread there. Then I cross a busy road and wait for the lights. After that, the
street is quiet. I like this part best. There are tall trees on both sides. In
summer, the trees are full of birds. In winter, the branches are bare and the
wind is cold. When I reach the office, I buy a coffee and read my email. Then I
start work. The morning walk helps me think.
