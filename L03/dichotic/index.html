Dichotic Listening

You will hear two voices at the same time — a male voice in your left ear and a female voice in your right ear — for about one minute.

Your job is to shadow one voice: repeat what it says, word for word, as best you can. Ignore the other voice. The clip is short — about 48 seconds.

Headphones required. Without stereo headphones the two voices blend and the demo will not work. Do it somewhere you can speak aloud (or whisper).
