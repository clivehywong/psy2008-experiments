Posner's Spatial Cueing

Keep your eyes on the central +. An arrow appears above the cross, pointing left or right. Then a yellow square appears on the left or the right. Press the key for the side the square appears on — as fast as you can.

Keep your hands ready: F = left, J = right (or tap the buttons on a phone). Keep your eyes on the cross — do not look at the arrow, and do not move your eyes to the target.

30 trials · about 3 minutes. The arrow is sometimes correct, sometimes wrong, sometimes neutral (a double-headed arrow).
