Posner's Spatial Cueing

Keep your eyes on the central +. A cue appears, then a yellow square appears on the left or the right. Press the key for the side the square appears on — as fast as you can.

Keep your hands ready: F = left, J = right (or tap the buttons on a phone). Eyes stay on the cross — do not move them to the cue.

30 trials · about 3 minutes. The cue is sometimes correct, sometimes wrong, sometimes neutral.
