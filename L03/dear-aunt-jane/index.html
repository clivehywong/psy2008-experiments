Dear Aunt Jane

You will hear two voices, one in each ear, saying a few words quickly. The words from the two ears are interleaved in time.

Headphones required. Your task, while it plays: shadow the voice in your LEFT ear — repeat it aloud (or under your breath) word for word, and keep going.
