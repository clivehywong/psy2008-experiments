Schneider & Shiffrin (1977)

You will memorise 1–4 characters. Then a series of frames flashes up on the screen. Each frame holds four characters. Press SPOT as soon as you see a character from your memory set.

How to play: press SPACE (or tap the big button) the moment a memory-set character appears. Frames are fast on purpose — speed is the whole point. You will play two blocks: one where the target characters are always the same kind, and one where they change from trial to trial.

12 trials · about 2 minutes.
