Serial Position Curve

You will study two lists of 20 words, one word at a time. After each list, write down as many words as you can remember, in any order.

The two lists differ in one way: after the second list you will count backwards by 3s for 30 seconds before recalling. Watch what that does to the end of the list.

2 lists · about 5 minutes. Spelling doesn't have to be perfect.
