Semantic Priming

On each trial, two letter strings appear, one above the other. Decide as fast as you can whether both are real words.

Keys: J = YES (both are words), F = NO (at least one is not a word) — or tap the buttons. Some pairs are related in meaning (bread / wheat); some are not (chair / money).

40 trials · about 3 minutes.

YES — both words (J)
NO — a nonword (F)
