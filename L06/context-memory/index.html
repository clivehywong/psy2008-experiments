Context-Dependent Memory

You will study four short lists of words. Each list is studied in one environment — Underwater or Beach — and then recalled in an environment, which may or may not match.

This is the logic of Godden & Baddeley's (1975) diving experiment: study words on land or underwater, then test on land or underwater. Just read each word as it appears; you'll be asked to recall straight afterwards.

4 lists · about 6 minutes. Watch the background — that is the context.
