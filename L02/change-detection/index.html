Spot the Change

Two versions of a real-world scene will alternate with a brief grey screen. One object changes.

Click or tap the object that changes as soon as you find it. There are 4 scenes. You have 30 seconds per scene.

Your results

Why was that hard? The grey flash wipes out the motion signal that normally tells you where something changed. Without that cue, you must search the scene object by object — with attention. That is change blindness: perception is not a photograph; it is built from whatever you attend.
