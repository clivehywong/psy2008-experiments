The Donders Experiment (1868)

Can you measure how long a decision takes — in your own head?

Round 1 — Simple RT: a dot appears in the centre. Tap the screen (or press any key) as fast as you can. 5 trials.

Round 2 — Choice RT: the dot appears on the LEFT or RIGHT. Tap that side of the screen (or press A = left, L = right). 5 trials.

⚠️ Don't respond before the dot appears — premature responses don't count!
