PSY2008 Cognitive Psychology

In-class experiments, organised by lecture. Open on your phone or laptop — no login, nothing is uploaded.

L1 · Introduction — How Do We Know a Mind Is in There?

The Donders Experiment (1868) Simple vs choice reaction time — measure your own decision time.

L2 · Perception — The Impossible Problem Your Brain Solves for Free

Ames Room A distorted room tricks your size perception — the same retinal image, two sizes. Spot the Change A flickering scene hides one change — find out what your attention misses. Apparent Motion Sequential blinks become a moving light — see how timing builds motion. Structure from Motion A flat cloud of dots spins into a 3D ring — depth from motion alone. Ames Room & The Ambiguous Retinal Image Walk across a distorted room and see why one image could be a thousand objects.

L3 · Attention — The Filter and Its Leaks

The Stroop Task Name the ink, ignore the word — measure your own Stroop effect. Dichotic Listening Two voices, one per ear — shadow one and discover what your filter throws away. (Headphones!) Dear Aunt Jane The 1960 cross-ear illusion: neither ear makes sense alone — but together they do. (Headphones!) Posner's Spatial Cueing A cue moves your attention before your eyes — measure your own covert spotlight. Schneider & Shiffrin (1977) Hold characters in memory and spot them in flashing frames — why practice makes it automatic.

L4 · Short-Term & Working Memory — The Workbench

Digit Span How many digits can you hold? Find your own break point.
