Luck & Vogel (1997) — Change Detection

On each trial you will see a set of coloured squares for 100 ms, then a blank screen, then the squares again. Your task: decide whether the second display is the same or different.

Keep your eyes on the centre. If a square changes, it changes colour and nothing else. Answer by tapping the buttons or pressing S (same) / D (different).

20 trials (sets of 3, 4, 6 or 8 squares) · about 3 minutes.
