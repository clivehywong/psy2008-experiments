Brown-Peterson Task

On each trial you will see three letters and a three-digit number. Then you must count backwards aloud by threes from the number until you are asked to recall the letters.

Counting aloud is essential. It is what stops you rehearsing the letters — say the numbers out loud (or clearly under your breath). Then type the three letters.

9 trials (delays of 3, 9 and 18 seconds) · about 3 minutes. Letters may be recalled in any order.
