Sperling (1960) — Iconic Memory

You will see a 3 × 4 grid of letters flash up briefly, then disappear. There are three kinds of trial:

  • Whole report — type as many letters as you can from the whole grid.
  • Partial report — a tone tells you which row to report: high = top, medium = middle, low = bottom.
  • Delayed partial report — the tone comes a second later, after the grid has faded.
Plan: ① learn the three tones (played long) → ② 3 practice trials with a slow display and feedback → ③ the real 9 trials. About 5 minutes. Note: browsers can't flash reliably for the original 50 ms — in the real trials the grid shows for 150 ms.

Step 1 — Learn the tones

Each row of the grid has its own tone. Listen to all three; they are played longer here than in the real trials.

High tone → report the TOP row
Medium tone → report the MIDDLE row
Low tone → report the BOTTOM row
